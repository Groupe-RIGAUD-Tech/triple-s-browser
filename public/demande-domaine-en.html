2. Advantages of the Triple S Network

  • No more passwords: users authenticate with their local encrypted vault (unique key per domain) and a temporary 2FA code. No secret is ever stored on your server.
  • Eliminates phishing: cryptographic keys are bound to the verified domain by the browser; a fake site cannot reuse them.
  • Zero-JS Shield: scripts and <iframe> tags are blocked natively, neutralizing most common web attacks.
  • Protection against rogue AI agents: with no scripts or persistent sessions, no automated agent can act behind the user's back. Every action requires explicit human approval.
  • Built for the post-quantum era: authentication already combines Ed25519 and ML-DSA-65 (NIST standard) to resist future quantum computers.
  • Future-ready security: our goal is to continuously strengthen protocol security and sovereignty without sacrificing simplicity.

3. Trade-offs & Limitations

Using a .sss domain involves deliberate technical constraints. While they may limit certain marketing practices, they provide a direct safety and trust advantage for visitors:

  • No marketing ads: third-party ad networks and banners do not run on Triple S.
  • No client tracking or analytics: no cookies, tracking pixels, or behavioral statistics tools.
  • Fewer dynamic automations: complex forms, live chats, and JavaScript animations are disabled. Ideal for clean, direct websites, though heavily dynamic sites require adaptation.

We are actively working on native, sovereign solutions to gradually replace these current web features without ever compromising security.

🛡️ Mandatory Content Policy (Child-Safe)

All content published on a .sss domain must be safe to read and view by a child. 18+ photos or videos, pornography, and explicit violence are strictly forbidden. Any violation results in immediate removal from the directory.
